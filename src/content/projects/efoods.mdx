## The problem

Ordering, cooking and delivering food involves four kinds of users who all need live, accurate information.

## Approach

- Architected a platform of 4 independent services behind an API gateway
- Containerised every service with Docker and orchestrated them with Kubernetes
- Role-based access for Admin, Customer, Restaurant and Rider
- Real-time order tracking with the Google Maps and Geocode APIs
- Payments through Stripe and PayHere
